ChatMuse

Supporting In-Person Small-Group Conversation Experience with a Proactive Assistive AI Agent in Mixed Reality

Shaoze Zhou, Joaquin Frangi, Diana Nelly Rivera Rodriguez, Rawan Alghofaili, Janet G. Johnson, Lingyao Li, Renkai Ma, Christine Lisetti, and Chen Chen

Read the paperDOIWatch video

Support in the flow of conversation.

ChatMuse explores how a proactive AI agent can help people engage in small-group conversations. It interprets the ongoing conversation and provides real-time suggestions for what to say and how to respond through a mixed reality interface.
